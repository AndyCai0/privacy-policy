We take your personal information and privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use the App, how we use and store it, which third parties it may be shared with, and what rights you have.

Please read this Policy carefully before using the App. By creating an account or using the App, you acknowledge that you have read and agree to this Policy. If you do not agree with any part of this Policy, please stop using the App.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to provide the service.

1.1 Account information

  • Email address — used for registration, login, password recovery, and verification codes.
  • Password / credentials — securely managed by our third-party authentication provider, Supabase Auth. We do not store or read your password in plaintext.
  • Account identifier (User ID) — automatically generated to keep each account's data separate.

1.2 Business data you enter

The App is a parcel and package-consolidation management tool. Content you enter is saved, including but not limited to:

  • Package information — tracking numbers, customer reference numbers, forwarded tracking numbers, forwarding company, lookup website, nicknames, notes, and specifications such as freight/insurance cost, weight, volume, and dimensions;
  • Domestic parcel information — domestic courier tracking numbers, shopping-platform order/transaction numbers, purchase platform, warehouse, free-storage period, and notes;
  • Item and inventory information — brand, product name, quantity, declared value/unit price, weight, ratings, tags, and inventory notes;
  • Logistics tracking information — carrier events (time, location, status) retrieved and displayed for you.

1.3 Feedback information

When you submit feedback through the in-app form, we collect the feedback content, your contact email, and, to help diagnose the issue, the App version and device information (device model, OS version).

1.4 Photos and screenshots

When you use the screenshot-recognition feature, the App reads screenshots from your photo library with your permission for content recognition. Recognition runs on your device locally, or in Apple's private compute environment (see "AI Features" below). We do not upload your entire photo library or use it for any purpose other than recognition.

1.5 What we do not collect

  • The App integrates no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs, and performs no behavioral event tracking or cross-app tracking.
  • The App's data model contains no dedicated fields for a recipient's real name, home address, government ID, or bank card number. (If you voluntarily enter such information into free-text fields like notes, please be mindful of the risk.)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide account registration, login, and authentication;
  • To save and synchronize the data you enter across your devices;
  • To retrieve and display logistics tracking for you;
  • To provide AI-assisted recognition and data-entry features;
  • To respond to and handle the feedback you submit;
  • To keep the service secure, troubleshoot issues, and prevent abuse.

3. Storage and Cloud Sync

  • After you sign in, the data you enter is synchronized and stored on the cloud servers of our cloud provider Supabase, to enable multi-device sync and backup.
  • The storage region is: Singapore. If your region differs from the storage region, by using the App you consent to the cross-border/cross-region data transfer necessary to enable cloud sync.
  • We use mechanisms such as Supabase Row-Level Security (RLS) to restrict each account to accessing only its own (or explicitly authorized) data.
  • You may also use the App without signing in, in which case data is stored only on your device and is not uploaded to the cloud.

4. Third-Party Sharing and Data Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. Your information is transmitted to third parties only in the following necessary cases:

4.1 Cloud service (Supabase)

Used for authentication, database storage, and backend functions. Your account and business data are stored here.

4.2 Third-party logistics lookup websites

To display logistics tracking, the App and our servers send tracking numbers to the relevant carrier/consolidation lookup websites (for example 5566cx.com, xjgjwl666.com) to obtain tracking information. These websites have their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review as needed.

4.3 Data flow related to AI features

See Section 5. When you actively choose to send data to a third-party AI, that data leaves the App.

Except for the cases above and as required by law, we do not provide your personal information to any other third party.

5. AI Features

The App offers several AI-assisted features. Please be aware of their data flows:

  1. On-device recognition (Apple Intelligence) — screenshot and text recognition runs by default locally on your device using Apple's on-device models; the data does not leave your device.
  2. Prompts you manually give to a third-party AI — the App generates copyable prompts that may include data such as your tracking numbers and product names. Whether to send them, and to which third-party AI (e.g. ChatGPT, Claude, Doubao, Kimi), is entirely your choice and a manual action. Once you paste such content into a third-party AI, that data becomes subject to that third party's privacy policy and is no longer controlled by the App. Please assess the risk before doing so.
  3. Browser-agent product-name lookup — the App can generate instructions for an assistant in your own browser to look up orders/product names on shopping sites. Any such fetching is performed by your browser; the App itself does not access your shopping-site accounts.

6. Collaboration and Multi-Account Sharing

The App provides collaboration/sharing features: one account can authorize another account to access its data. Once you grant access to someone, the authorized party can view the data you shared. Only grant access to people you trust, and revoke it when necessary.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

  • We retain your data while you use the service; you may delete or edit the content you entered at any time.
  • Data export — you can export your data as a JSON file via "Settings → Export Backup" to keep your own copy.
  • Account deletion — you can request account deletion via "Settings → Delete Account". A 7-day cooling-off period follows, during which you can cancel the request simply by signing back in. Once the period ends, we delete the data and authentication records associated with your account on the server; this cannot be undone. Please export a backup beforehand.
  • Where laws or regulations require longer retention, we will handle data accordingly.

8. Your Rights

To the extent permitted by applicable law, you have the right to:

  • Access and correct your personal information (edit it directly in the App);
  • Export your data (backup export feature);
  • Delete your data or close your account;
  • Withdraw consent (e.g. revoke photo access, revoke collaboration authorization, sign out);
  • Complain or inquire about the processing of your personal information (see "Contact Us").

9. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encryption in transit (HTTPS), credential management handled by Supabase Auth, and access isolation based on Row-Level Security. However, please understand that no method of transmission over the Internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 14 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). If you are a minor, please use the App under the guidance of a guardian. If we learn that we have collected a child's personal information without guardian consent, we will delete it as soon as possible.
